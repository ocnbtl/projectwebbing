# Madagin

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners whose work has moved beyond their current website. They need to understand the studio's capabilities, inspect real projects, and prepare a useful inquiry.

## Product Purpose

A founder-led web studio combining strategy, design, and development in one collaboration. The public site demonstrates that work and supports a project brief.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js public pages, published projects and articles, and authenticated internal publishing. Preserve publishing behavior and content integrity. Inquiry delivery is not verified; the current brief is saved locally and must never claim to have been sent. No invented customers, testimonials, awards, or measured outcomes.

## Brand Commitments

The public name is Madagin. The user's exact blue Balsa water background persists across the public site. Tall, optically spaced glass letters emerge from water with illuminated edges and local color ripples. Six complete phrases tell the story from ideas to connection, each supported by an interaction. No black fade, rotating superlatives, or project-preview cursor. Work and About comprise primary navigation; “Bring an idea” opens a focused inquiry preparation flow. Presence combines a labeled 10–40 participant simulation with real anonymous Cloudflare cursor connections. Above 40 real connections the main count becomes live and simulation stops. Counts represent connections, not verified unique humans. Navigation and content remain usable without effects.

## Evidence on Hand

Published Sage Burress and Masonry Color Corrections projects and their existing captured website images. Published article: Before a website rebuild, gather the right things. No approved testimonials or quantified outcomes supplied.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Normal scrolling, keyboard navigation, intentional touch equivalents, readable HTML without JavaScript, reduced-motion and limited-capability fallbacks. Never require a cursor effect to discover the work.
