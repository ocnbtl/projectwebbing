# Madagin voice and editorial direction v0.6

**Status:** `DIRECTION PROPOSED`  
**Decision owner:** Ocean  
**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Primary mode:** `CLIENT`  

## 1. Executive summary

The previous concept board failed because it treated strategy language as finished website copy. It introduced explanatory slogans, abstract transitions, and boxed value statements that sounded manufactured instead of sounding like a confident founder-led studio.

The new direction keeps the four parts Ocean endorsed and removes the rest:

- **Promise:** Sites people remember, trust, and choose.
- **Name origin:** Madagin comes from **made again**.
- **Method:** Fresh perspective → strategic direction → intentional transformation → distinctive presence.
- **Standards:** Distinctive, resonant, trusted, compelling.

The proposed site voice is sparse, direct, and commercially grounded. Its central tension is not “transformation” as an abstract idea. It is the recognizable moment when a business has moved forward and its website has not.

### Proposed public hierarchy

1. **Promise:** Sites people remember, trust, and choose.
2. **Category line:** Strategy, design, and development for businesses that have outgrown the way they show up online.
3. **Commercial tension:** The business moved forward. The website didn't.
4. **Name story:** Madagin comes from **made again**. We rethink and rebuild how your business shows up online—so the site catches up to the work.
5. **Point of view:** The website is the version of your business most people meet first.
6. **Method:** The four retained stages, written as actions rather than slogans.
7. **Standards:** The four retained words, expressed as review questions.
8. **Proof and conversion:** Approved work, evidence, and a direct project path must eventually carry the close.

## 8. Non-goals

- Invent another concept name.
- Publish a manifesto about transformation.
- Turn the method or standards into generic value cards.
- Claim the work is memorable, trusted, or compelling without proof.
- Copy Noomo's pixels/glass, Studio Freight's freight metaphors, Réplica's identity, or Il Capo's palette.
- Produce a complete homepage without approved case-study evidence.

## 9. Constraints and guardrails

| Guardrail | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Meaning before effect | Every large visual gesture must map to name, hierarchy, work, or decision-making |
| One idea per scene | No section stacks a slogan, an abstract subtitle, and a second explanation |
| Work before self-celebration | Approved cases must eventually replace claims about Madagin's quality |
| Plain words around owned words | “Fresh perspective” and “distinctive” may stay; their explanations must be concrete |
| No false scale | Use founder-led accountability, not agency-team language |
| Proposal state | Palette, typography, layout, and copy remain `AWAITING DECISION` |

## 18. Plain-English summary

Madagin should stop explaining transformation and start with a problem prospective clients already recognize: the business has moved forward and the website has not. The name **made again** then becomes the answer, not another slogan. The new direction uses the approved promise, method, and standards with far less copy: one strong statement per scene, action-based method language, standards written as questions, and a restrained editorial presentation that leaves room for real work to prove the claims. Ocean owns approval; nothing here is final or published.
