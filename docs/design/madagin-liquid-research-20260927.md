# Madagin: liquid specimen research and execution decisions

September 27, 2026. Design research for the requested replacement site, not a continuation packet.

## Observed references

- [Igloo Inc.](https://www.igloo.inc/) was inspected live in the browser. A pale translucent object, edge highlights, and a very restrained field make material the subject. The sparse accessibility tree and long visual introduction are cautionary: Madagin's navigation and offer must exist immediately in HTML. Awwwards' [2024 winners announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/awwwards_we-are-thrilled-to-announce-that-the-winners-activity-7307431367728861187-ED07) identifies Igloo as Site of the Year and Developer Site of the Year. The current live version is not asserted to be the exact awarded build.
- [Terminal Industries](https://terminal-industries.com/) was inspected live. The opening uses a wide cinematic truck composition, strong framing, and clear product actions. Borrow the deliberate camera and material lighting, not its large menu or logistics vocabulary. The studio's [Site of the Month announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rejouice_were-proud-to-share-that-ourterminal-industries-activity-7381770665009270785-oOgh) supports its award context; the Awwwards detail page timed out during this inspection.
- [Studio Freight](https://studiofreight.com/) was inspected live. A white field, restrained navigation, scattered rectangular work images, and a centered statement give work priority over chrome. Borrow the confidence of scale changes and real imagery. Do not copy its composition, serif voice, or slogan. Its work also appears in [Awwwards' 2023 publication](https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/gallery/2023/07/HOT-RIGHT-NOW-BOOK-2023.pdf); no specific year/month award is claimed here.

## Editorial and design checks

[NN/G's 2024 tool evaluation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-design-tools-not-ready/) contains examples of generic, similar wireframes despite different briefs. This is dated evidence about those tools, not a detector of AI authorship or a claim about all current tools. For this site: each section must serve a different question; remove reusable agency filler; use actual project subjects; avoid repeating a card grid and numbered slogan grammar. The known line “strategy, design, and development” is useful because it identifies the offer, but needs real work directly after it.

Copy test: say who the work is for, what changes, and how to proceed. A phrase such as “elevate your digital presence” fails because it could describe anything. “A website that catches up to your business” describes the confirmed client situation. A service ambition must not imply a guaranteed commercial outcome. No fictional social proof.

## Directions compared

1. Liquid specimen: white material wordmark, typographic collection, asymmetric project proof. Selected because it fulfills the specific opening and lets the work supply color.
2. Kinetic type catalogue: monochrome poster and rapidly changing faces. Rejected as the main direction because motion could overwhelm the restrained material request.
3. Cinematic glass gallery: full-screen glass film with work inserts. Kept only as a supporting media idea because it would make navigation and loading depend too much on video.

## Implementation priorities and risks

1. Semantic HTML and ordinary routes first. One bounded hero renderer, no world imports. Real bevel geometry and physical glass lighting are the initial rendering approach; CSS is the readable fallback. Judge the material in motion at full width.
2. Individual arrival and settling, ripple response, and a portfolio pointer. Keep the native cursor until the custom pointer is ready; hide it over inputs and for touch/reduced motion.
3. Fixed descriptor stage and independently rotating verbs/nouns. Curated Cartesian vocabulary: express / reveal / shape and ideas / character / ambition. No outcome guarantees.
4. Existing project media and content, accessible work links, coordinated public route typography, truthful brief flow.
5. Pause, reduced motion, data-saving fallback, viewport visibility suspension, capped pixel density, frame-time and transfer inspection. Verify no old /world/ requests from the new homepage.
6. Typecheck, lint, production build, desktop/mobile motion capture, keyboard/history tests, and provider/alias verification before release.
