## 1. Production invariant

The same living subject must remain recognizable from seed through full flowering tree. The environment, root/trunk/branch topology, time-of-day progression, physical camera logic, and color story must behave like one directed production—not unrelated AI clips edited together.

The transformation is metaphorical time compression, not a horticultural documentary. Production must not name a cultivar or imply botanical accuracy until a botanical reference owner approves the exact species/cultivar and propagation premise.

## 5. Subject topology and identity lock

Create four approved anchor frames before any motion generation:

1. `A0-SEED`: exact seed shape, size reference, shell markings, soil granularity, camera axis.
2. `A1-ROOT-SPROUT`: seed position, primary root fork, first stem curve, soil cross-section.
3. `A2-STRUCTURE`: trunk silhouette, three primary branches, canopy footprint, horizon, light direction.
4. `A3-BLOSSOM`: same trunk and branch topology at maturity, blossom density, copy-safe side, ground/horizon.

Every generated shot must point to the applicable anchor-frame hash. If a later frame improves quality but changes topology, it starts a new branch in the manifest; it does not silently replace continuity.

### Topology rules

- Trunk leans 3–5 degrees toward frame right before correcting into the crown.
- Three primary branches: low-left, mid-right, upper-left; no perfectly symmetrical “logo tree.”
- Copy-safe region defaults to the upper-right third on landscape and the upper-center third on portrait; responsive variants may mirror composition only if branch identity remains clear.
- Root fork echoes the future branch distribution without becoming a literal circuit-board graphic.
- Blossom clusters reveal branch structure; they do not cover it completely.

## 6. Camera, lens, and motion language

Higgsfield currently documents start/end-frame workflows and explicit camera/lens controls. Treat those pages as provider capability evidence, not quality guarantees: [start/end-frame guide](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/A-Guide-to-Kling-Turbo-Start-End-Frame) and [camera control guide](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/ai-video-camera-control).

| Shot scale | Lens intent | Camera height / axis | Allowed movement | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seed / soil macro | 90–105mm macro character, shallow but readable focus | 5–10cm above soil, 10° downward | 2–4% slow dolly, restrained focus pull | Crash zoom, orbit, handheld wobble |
| Root cross-section | 50–65mm neutral compression | Perpendicular to soil plane | Slow lateral slide or near-static | Impossible underground volumetric fly-through |
| Sprout / trunk | 50mm natural perspective | Low but not heroic | Measured rise or tilt following growth | Whip tilt, speed ramp, drone language |
| Branch / bud | 85mm detail | Branch-height, stable horizon | Short track or rack focus | Macro-to-wide morph inside one shot |
| Full tree | 35–40mm restrained wide | Human eye height, stable axis | Very slow push or locked frame with natural air | Extreme wide distortion, 360° orbit |

Movement exists to reveal cause/effect and preserve spatial orientation. A shot fails if the camera move is more memorable than the subject or makes copy tracking difficult.

### Base continuity block

## 10. Generation and review sequence

1. Approve written meaning, species/cultivar status, literalness, and palette.
2. Create 12–20 compositionally different still candidates for A0; choose one, not an average.
3. Create A1–A3 from the approved prior anchor where the provider supports references; log every relationship.
4. Build a side-by-side topology contact sheet and reject silent branch/lighting drift.
5. Test only P0-01 and P0-05 motion at an economical setting first; assess camera, loop, safe zones, and artifact rate.
6. Test root-to-structure continuity with start/end frames.
7. Approve one desktop and one mobile composition family before broad generation.
8. Generate P1, then P2; edit, clean, grade, compress, and create posters outside the generation loop as appropriate.
9. Validate real browser delivery before calling any asset web-ready.

## 11. Acceptance rubric

| Dimension | Pass | Major reject | Minor repair candidate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subject identity | Seed/trunk/branch topology matches anchors | Different tree, branch count, silhouette, seed | Small twig/petal density cleanup |
| Botany/material | Credible bark, root, petal, soil behavior at intended realism | Fused petals, plastic bark, impossible root/soil | Local texture or color cleanup |
| Environment | Same soil, horizon, background, season | Location or season change | Small background artifact |
| Light/color | Direction and time progression match bible | Shadow flips, grade jump, clipped pink | Gradeable exposure/color offset |
| Camera | Intended axis, lens character, move and speed | Unplanned orbit/zoom/wobble | Crop or stabilization repair |
| Copy safety | Required zone remains stable and legible | Subject crosses text area unpredictably | Minor mask/crop correction |
| Motion | Physically coherent; start/end settle | Morphing, acceleration spike, loop jump | Small timing/retime repair |
| AI artifact | No prominent tell at target display size | Anatomy/topology/text/watermark artifact | Paintable isolated defect |
| Rights/provenance | Manifest complete; inputs and terms verified | Missing or incompatible rights record | Administrative field recoverable before use |

Any major reject fails the shot. Three repair passes without acceptance triggers a method change: still/composite, practical footage, different model, or a simpler scene.

Third-party model policies can be more restrictive. Therefore:

- Record the chosen underlying model and its provider policy/version.

This is a production control, not legal advice or a rights determination.
