# Liquid identity assets

The wordmark is code-authored geometry made for Madagin. `tools/build-liquid-letters.mjs` uses the Helvetiker bold outlines bundled with Three.js, computes a rounded implicit surface, and packs the result into `letters.bin` plus `letters.json`. The source font and its license are included. No third-party 3D object is embedded.

The runtime uses Three.js physical transmission, procedural studio lighting, localized water ripples, soft contact shadows, and a restrained reflected silhouette. The surface remains an art-directed approximation of glass and water. The services field is a code-authored SVG light filter. No generated client work or implied client results are used.

Manrope, Bodoni Moda, and Bricolage Grotesque are supplied by `next/font/google` and self-hosted by the build. Their OFL licenses are included in this directory. Source: the respective `ofl/manrope`, `ofl/bodonimoda`, and `ofl/bricolagegrotesque` directories in https://github.com/google/fonts.

The direction study in the local `output/madagin-liquid-20260927` folder is a private generated composition reference, not a public portfolio asset. Public project images retain their existing website context and provenance under `public/media/projects`.
